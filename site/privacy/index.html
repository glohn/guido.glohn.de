Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026. This translation is provided for convenience; the German version is authoritative.

1. Controller

Guido Lohn
Email: kontakt@glohn.de

2. Overview

This website is a personal contact page. It sets no cookies, uses no local browser storage, uses no analytics or tracking tools and loads no content from third-party servers (no external fonts, scripts or images).

Your browser only keeps downloaded files in its cache for a short time and stores, for one year, the instruction to access this site only via encrypted HTTPS (HSTS). The contact card and the QR image are only stored if you download them yourself.

Two small scripts run entirely in your browser and transmit no data: one shows, based on the current date, whether a certification has expired; the other keeps the screen on while the QR page is open.

3. Hosting and delivery of the website

The website is hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS). The files are stored in Amazon S3 in Frankfurt am Main, Germany, and delivered through the Amazon CloudFront content delivery network. The provider is Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL, 38 Avenue John F. Kennedy, L-1855 Luxembourg.

When you open the site, AWS processes the data technically required to do so: your IP address, date and time, the requested file, the amount of data transferred, your browser identifier (user agent) and, where applicable, the previously visited page. The purpose is to deliver the website and keep it stable and secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is providing the site securely and reliably.

I have not enabled the access logs of CloudFront and S3; I do not store any access data myself. AWS processes the data on my behalf under a data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR (AWS Data Processing Addendum).

CloudFront delivers the site from servers in Europe, Israel, Türkiye, the United States, Canada and Mexico, and occasionally from other locations worldwide; requests are usually answered by a server close to you. For the United States (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795; Amazon Web Services, Inc. is certified under it), Canada and Israel, adequacy decisions of the European Commission apply. Otherwise, the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914) apply, which are part of the AWS Data Processing Addendum.

4. Contact card and QR code

The contact card (vCard) and the QR code are delivered like all other files of this site (section 3). No further data is processed.

5. Links to other services

The site links to LinkedIn, GitHub and Credly. The respective site is only opened when you click a link; from then on, that provider's privacy policy applies. No data is transferred to these services beforehand. When you click a link, the destination is also not told that you came from this site.

6. Contact by email

If you send me an email, I process your email address, your name if provided, the content of your message and the technical details of the email (for example time and headers) in order to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is answering your request.

Emails to addresses at glohn.de are received through Amazon Simple Email Service (SES) in Frankfurt am Main, automatically checked for spam and malware, and stored in Amazon S3 in Frankfurt am Main. A program (AWS Lambda, also in Frankfurt am Main) forwards them to my personal mailbox with an email provider in Germany. Messages classified as spam are marked in the subject line; messages containing malware are not forwarded. Section 3 applies to AWS accordingly.

The forwarding program logs an internal identifier, the result and the size of each message, and in case of errors also the error message. If a message cannot be forwarded, I receive a notice with sender, recipient, subject and date instead.

The copy in Amazon S3 is deleted automatically after 90 days (for technical reasons possibly a few days later), the logs after 30 days. I delete emails in my mailbox as soon as they are no longer needed for your request and further communication, at the latest two years after the last contact.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17) and restriction of processing (Art. 18). Please contact kontakt@glohn.de.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for me is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Rhineland-Palatinate (Der Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit Rheinland-Pfalz), Hintere Bleiche 34, 55116 Mainz, Germany, www.datenschutz.rlp.de.

8. Right to object

Where I process your data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21(1) GDPR). An informal email to kontakt@glohn.de is sufficient.

9. Provision of your data

Providing your data is neither a statutory nor a contractual requirement. Without your IP address the site cannot be delivered; without your sender address I cannot reply to you. There is no automated decision-making, including profiling.
